Privacy Policy

Loose Ends by Critter Forge Games · Last updated: October 2, 2026

Summary

Loose Ends does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics run by us.

What the game stores

Your game progress (level, points, settings, chosen theme and language) is saved only on your device, in the browser or app storage. It never leaves your device unless you play on a platform that offers cloud saves (see below). You can delete it at any time with “Reset progress” in the game settings or by clearing the app data.

Versions on other platforms

The game is also published on third-party platforms (such as Telegram, Playgama, CrazyGames, Yandex Games and itch.io). There, the platform may save your progress to its cloud and may show ads through its own advertising service. Any data processed in that case is handled by the platform under its own privacy policy. The Microsoft Store version and the version at critterforgegames.github.io contain no ads and no third-party tracking.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
