How it works

Send a link and your art

A Sandbox or portal link to the playable game, your icon, and one piece of key art if you have it. No art at all is fine too, I will work from the game itself.

I play your game, pick three moments that look impossible at first glance, record them and set the hook, timer and end card. Covers are cut and lettered for each size.

Covers as PNG, three MP4 files at 1080×1920, a text file with titles, descriptions and tags. One round of changes is included.
